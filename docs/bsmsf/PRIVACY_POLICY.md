# Privacy Policy for Better SMS Forward

Last updated: December 3, 2025

## Overview
Better SMS Forward is a simple SMS forwarding application that runs locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

## Permissions Used

### SMS Permissions (RECEIVE_SMS, SEND_SMS)
- **Purpose**: To receive incoming SMS messages and forward them to your configured destination number
- **Data Usage**: SMS content is only forwarded to the number you specify. No messages are stored or sent to external servers.

### Phone State (READ_PHONE_STATE)
- **Purpose**: To detect available SIM cards and manage SMS sending functionality
- **Data Usage**: Used only to determine which SIM card to use for sending messages. No phone state data is collected or transmitted.

### Battery Optimization (REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS)
- **Purpose**: To ensure the app continues running in the background to forward SMS messages
- **Data Usage**: No data collection. This permission only requests exemption from battery optimization.

## Data Storage
All app settings (destination number, preferences) are stored locally on your device only. No data is transmitted to external servers or third parties.

## Third-Party Services
This app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy
This app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date.
